Know where you are in the song.

A practice companion that helps you hear, follow and play through whole songs, take your own part in the band, and keep everyone on the same bar when you play together.

See band sessions What comes first

Lost in the second chorus?

Most practice tools give you something to play over. They don't teach you to find your way through a piece. But knowing the form is what lets you come in on time, recover when you get lost, and end together with the band.

What we're building

Follow the form

See the whole song as a timeline of sections. The current bar is always highlighted, and a cue comes before every change.

Take a role

Hear the full band, then mute the bass, the chords or the melody and play that part yourself, with the rest of the band behind you.

Practise your way

Slow it down or move it to your key without losing your place. Count-in, metronome and loops for the part you want to repeat.

Play together

Start a band session and every phone or tablet in the room shows the same section and bar. Nobody gets left behind.

Band sessions

Everyone in the band, on the same bar.

The hardest moment in a jam is getting lost: are we in the second chorus or the third? Is the ending coming? With a shared timeline, anyone who loses their place can glance down, find the next landmark, and come back in, without stopping the band.

  • No typing of addresses, no accounts: devices nearby find the session.
  • Join a running song at any time and jump straight to the right bar.
  • Each device decides for itself: click, backing parts, or just the timeline.
  • Play along with the app's clock, or let the leader tap the section changes of a live band.

Works on the same Wi-Fi, or on the leader's phone hotspot. No internet needed in the rehearsal room.

Roadmap

Order, not dates. The first version focuses on one style and one form, a 12-bar blues, to get the core experience right.

Now

  • Follow the form of a 12-bar blues
  • Mute a part and play it yourself
  • Tempo, key, count-in and loops

Next

  • Band sessions: every device on the same bar
  • More forms, styles and grooves

Later

  • A guided learning path from listening to jamming
  • Real repertoire, once licensing is sorted
  • Sessions over the internet, teacher and ensemble plans
